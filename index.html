Capstone Project

Detecting fake hotel reviews with NLP and a booking check

We trained a classifier to tell genuine hotel reviews from deceptive ones, and put a booking check in front of it so only reviews from verified reservations reach the model. It started as a capstone project in a group of three and was later published at IEEE ICECCT 2021.
